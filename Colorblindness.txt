Colorblindness is the decreased ability to see color or differences/shades of it. Severity can
end up being anywhere between unnoticeable to complete absence of color perception.

Colorblindness is more apparent in men, with 8% of men experiencing some form of colorblindness compared
to 0.5% of women. It happens due to a problem/variation in the cone cells located in a person's retina.
The most common form of colorblindness is called "congenital red-green colorblindness."

Depending on jurisdiction, colorblindness can hinder people's job opportunities without proper accomodation.
Those that have some form of it are ineligible for careers as pilots, drivers, officers, firefighters, and
members of the armed forces. However, there are still a number of ways they can still live in spite of that.
