You can enter values from 0 to 255. These are the values that are valid in RGB values.
Anything outside of this range is considered invalid, and will not be considered in the final calculations.
You can continue to enter values outside of the range until you enter three valid numbers.

Words and letters do not work as well, as it does not know how to translate it. Please be mindful of that
as you continue to use this program. Thank you.
